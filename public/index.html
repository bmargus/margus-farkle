📖 Rules & scoring (tap to expand)

Roll six dice. Set aside scoring dice, then bank your points or risk rolling the remaining dice. If a roll scores nothing, you FARKLE and lose the points from that turn. If all six dice score, you get all six back to roll again. First player to the target wins.

Single 1 = 100; single 5 = 50. Three 1s = 1,000; three 2s = 200; three 3s = 300; three 4s = 400; three 5s = 500; three 6s = 600. Four/five/six of a kind double the triple score with each extra die. Straight 1–6 = 1,500; three pairs = 1,500; two triples = 2,500. Special combinations must be selected together on a single roll. Dice from separate rolls cannot form combinations.

Choose scoring dice by clicking them, then press Keep Selected Dice. Bank after keeping, or continue rolling. No minimum opening score required. No timer.
